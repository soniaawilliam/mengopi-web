# Agent guidance for Mengopi

## MVP data acquisition

For the MVP, build the directory from sources in this order of preference:

1. First-party submissions and partnerships with organisations, venues, councils, libraries, NGOs, community groups, and event organisers.
2. Official APIs and open datasets whose licences permit reuse, including relevant Malaysian public datasets.
3. Community submissions and corrections, subject to moderation and verification before publication.
4. Carefully limited crawling only where the source permits automated access and reuse.

Treat crawled data as unverified leads rather than automatically publishable content. Prefer storing factual fields and a link to the source over copying descriptions, reviews, menus, photographs, logos, or other expressive content. Do not scrape behind logins or paywalls, bypass CAPTCHAs or access controls, or use a platform's content to build a competing directory where its terms prohibit that use. Respect applicable terms, robots guidance, rate limits, attribution requirements, takedown requests, and data-protection obligations.

Every imported or submitted record should retain its source URL, source type, licence or permission basis where known, date accessed, last-verified date, and verification status. Public listings should make provenance and freshness visible to users.

## Artwork and images

Use only artwork, photographs, illustrations, icons, logos, and other images that the project has explicit permission to use or that are available under a licence permitting the intended use. Do not use AI-generated artwork or images. Prefer genuinely free-to-use or permissively licensed assets. Record the asset's source, creator, licence, attribution text, and source URL, and display attribution wherever the licence requires it. Do not copy images from directory listings or search results merely because they are publicly viewable.

## Product direction

The MVP should prioritise a trustworthy, accessible, list-first directory for free and affordable food, clothing, community resources, and social events. Include cost, location, eligibility where relevant, opening or event dates, source, and verification state. Keep browsing account-free and minimise collection of personal data.
